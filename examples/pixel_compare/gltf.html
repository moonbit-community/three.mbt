three.js vs three.mbt: GLTFLoader pixel comparison

Each scene loads a glTF model with upstream's GLTFLoader (Draco through DRACOLoader; animated models posed at a fixed time with an AnimationMixer) and renders it at 128×96 with upstream's WebGPURenderer (r187), then does the same with the MoonBit port's loader and renderer (gltf/main.mbt). Both frames are read back with readRenderTargetPixelsAsync and compared exactly. Models load from .repos/three.js/examples/models/gltf. Results are also in window.__pixelResults.
